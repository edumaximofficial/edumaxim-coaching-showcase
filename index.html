How it works

From answer key to rank list in one morning

Everything a coaching teacher does after a mock test, on one phone. No OMR scanner, no spreadsheets to total by hand.

1

Give the answer key in one line

Your question paper is already printed? Type the key as letters, BADCC ABDCA…, or tap the bubbles. Add sections like 1-45 Physics, 46-90 Chemistry to get section-wise results.

  • Up to 200 questions, options A–D or A–E
  • NEET/JEE negative marking: ¼, ⅓, ½ or a full mark
  • Or write the questions and print a question paper from EduMaxim

2

Print the OMR sheets: blank, or with names

A clean A4 sheet with your institute's name and the EduMaxim logo. Print one blank sheet to photocopy, or one per student with the name printed and roll number already filled in. The black corner squares let a phone read the sheet from any angle.

  • Roll number grid, signature lines, filling instructions
  • Question paper PDF numbered to match

3

Photograph each sheet. Check. Save. Next.

The roll number finds the student, every bubble is read, and the score appears straight away. Faint, rubbed-out or double marks are highlighted, so the teacher checks only what needs checking. Save & scan next opens the camera again.

  • Works with tilted, sideways or upside-down photos and uneven classroom light
  • Two filled circles count as wrong
  • Already have an OMR machine? Upload its Excel export instead
  • Read on your own server: no AI service, no extra cost per sheet

Ranks for the batch. Growth for every student.

The rank list fills in as sheets are saved: ties share a rank, and every result shows where it came from. Parents get one alert with the score, then see their child's rank in the batch, section-wise bars, every answer against the key, and a growth graph across all tests.

  • Rank list in Excel with section-wise % and every answer
